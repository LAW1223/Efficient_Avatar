Efficient by design.

Two complementary techniques make the model cheaper to run: one reduces how many steps it takes, the other makes each step lighter.

  1. Original modelMiniMax H3, 40 denoising steps
  2. Step distillationLearn to generate in fewer steps
  3. 4-step model10× fewer denoising steps
  4. 4-bit attentionNVFP4 with rotation and calibration
  5. Efficient inferenceFaster attention in every step

Distillation

Video diffusion models build each clip by refining noise over many steps. The original model uses 40. Distillation trains a version that produces the video in just 4 steps.

Quantization

Attention is one of the most expensive operations in video generation. We compute it in 4-bit floating point (NVFP4), using rotation and calibration to keep rounding errors small, so the output stays close to 16-bit attention.
